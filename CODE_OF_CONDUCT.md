# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Contributing to a positive environment: empathy and kindness; respect for differing
opinions and experiences; constructive feedback given and accepted gracefully;
responsibility and apology for mistakes; focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour: sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual
attention; trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks;
public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without
explicit permission; other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and take appropriate, fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through the
repository's private channel (GitHub Security advisory, or a direct message to a
maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`). Complaints are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security are respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction** — private written warning, stating the violation.
2. **Warning** — warning with consequences for continued behaviour and a defined
   period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — temporary ban from interaction or public communication.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent ban from public interaction with the project.
