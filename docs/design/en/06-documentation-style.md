# Loreviva Documentation Standard

Write for readers who need to understand, create with, implement or adopt Loreviva. Begin with product goals and user situations, then explain the relationships, decisions and boundaries that form the product's logic.

## Organization

The overview explains positioning, value and experience; requirements define behavior, interactions and acceptance; architecture explains technical responsibilities and invariants; integration guides provide actionable workflows; the implementation plan organizes dependencies and delivery. Maintain one current state summary and keep detailed execution results in engineering records.

Integrate changes into the owning section and remove superseded wording. Keep version discussions, comparison research, authorization quotations, revision exchanges and execution logs in their respective internal records. Preserve original history, necessary sources and third-party copyright and license attribution. Editorial changes must not misrepresent provenance.

## Voice and Terminology

Use neutral, concrete product language. Identify creators, players, business customers, the platform or the system as the actor. Avoid conversational phrases such as “you asked,” “I suggest,” or “the AI thinks.” Retain AI, Agent and Harness when they accurately describe product capabilities.

Use descriptive headings and connected paragraphs. Avoid slogans, repetitive emphasis, revision labels and unnecessarily dense terminology. Use tables for comparable fields and steps for ordered actions. Keep world, character definition, actor instance, release, world instance, model connection and Skill terminology consistent.

## Design and Facts

Distinguish designed capabilities, implemented features, actual verification and commercial outcomes. Do not turn unverified capabilities into release promises or finite tests into whole-product approval. Place limitations where they affect a decision or action, and keep progress in state records rather than repeating disclaimers throughout the prose.

Editorial changes must preserve permissions, scope, numeric limits, interfaces, state machines, recovery semantics and acceptance requirements. Keep necessary detail in technical documents while making team and investor entrypoints explain product logic. The engineering contract remains the sole source of serialized fields.

## Languages and Delivery

Chinese and English share the same product logic, capability scope and state. English is a corresponding reading view written naturally rather than a literal rendering of discussion history. Check terminology, links, heading structure, numbers and interface identifiers after editing; behavior or technical changes require separate review.

Label exports with their actual version and development stage. Public delivery continues to verify provenance, licensing, private-data isolation and bilingual consistency. Internal records are not bundled with product prose.
