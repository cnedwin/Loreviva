# Design and Repository Support

Updated21 October2026.

Product design3.0.2 defines the full world-authoring, character, memory and customer-integration goals. The approved machine-contract baseline remains1.4.0 (58 operations,156 schemas, migrations0001–0007); this checkout now also contains the Task8 development snapshot, whose working contract is1.5.0 (65 operations,177 schemas, migrations0001–0008). The two must not be conflated: a working contract in the tree does not mean1.5.0 or complete Task8 is accepted. Reserved contract operations do not imply registered handlers.

The SDK, reference-client and self-memory governance implementations entered the tree with that snapshot after finite reviews; complete Task8,1.5.0 as a whole, Tasks9–12, sustained character growth and24 long-term experience scenarios are still not delivered as a complete product. Real-model quality, human experience, the complete installation release, payments and deployment remain unaccepted. Known failures and not-run items for this batch are listed in the[progress note](../../progress/2026-10-21.en.md); a documentation correction does not replace them.
